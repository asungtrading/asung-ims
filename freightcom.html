Every shipment booked with Freightcom through IMS. IMS reads each live shipment's status from Freightcom by itself (about every 3 hours, hourly when it is out for delivery) and Freightcom's charges four times a day. Shipments still on the way are always listed, whatever the dates. Late means not delivered a day after the expected date (pickup day + the carrier's transit days, weekends skipped — public holidays are not counted). Extra is what Freightcom billed beyond the quote, before tax. Amounts and credit are shown only to staff with freight booking or purchasing rights. Payment is made on the Freightcom site — IMS only shows it.
