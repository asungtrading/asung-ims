A portal account lets a customer sign in at tools.asung.ca/customer-portal.html and see its own sales, orders, reorder ideas and price list. One account can cover several customers — a head office and its stores. Prices on the portal are each store's own (its price tier, customer discount and deals — the same as a sales order). New accounts and password resets give a temporary password that is shown once — send it to the customer; the portal asks them to set their own. View as customer opens the portal as that account without its password (read only, 60 minutes, recorded below). Only staff with the Settings permission can use this page.
