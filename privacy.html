Asung IMS — Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · Asung Trading Inc.

1. Scope

This policy covers Asung IMS, the private internal system of Asung Trading Inc. (“Asung”, “we”), including its connection to Asung’s own QuickBooks Online company. Asung IMS is used only by Asung employees and people Asung has authorized.

2. What data Asung IMS handles

  • Business records Asung needs to run its operations: products, stock, purchase orders, sales orders, invoices, payments and credits.
  • Business contact details of Asung’s customers and suppliers (names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses).
  • Staff account details needed to sign in and to record who did what.
  • From QuickBooks Online: the accounting records of Asung’s own company that Asung IMS reads or writes (such as customers, products and services, invoices, payments, credit memos, accounts and tax codes), and the connection details Intuit issues when an administrator approves the connection.

3. How the data is used

Only to run Asung’s business: taking and shipping orders, managing stock, issuing invoices and keeping Asung’s books in step with its sales records. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except service providers that host or operate Asung IMS for us, or where the law requires it.

4. QuickBooks connection details

The connection details Intuit issues are kept in Asung’s private database. They are used only by Asung IMS to talk to Asung’s QuickBooks company, and are not shown on staff screens. When an administrator disconnects Asung IMS, these details are deleted and Asung IMS can no longer reach the QuickBooks company.

5. Access and security

Access to Asung IMS requires a signed-in Asung account, and each account sees only the functions its role allows. Data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

6. Retention

Business and accounting records are kept as long as Asung needs them to operate and as long as Canadian tax and business laws require.
