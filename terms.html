Asung IMS — Terms of Use

Effective October 29, 2026 · Asung Trading Inc.

1. What this application is

Asung IMS is the internal inventory, order and invoicing system of Asung Trading Inc. (“Asung”, “we”). It is a private application. It is not sold, licensed or offered to the public.

2. Who may use it

Only Asung employees and people Asung has authorized may sign in and use Asung IMS. Each user is responsible for keeping their sign-in details private and for the actions taken under their account.

3. Connection to QuickBooks Online

Asung IMS can connect to Asung’s own QuickBooks Online company. The connection is made only by an Asung QuickBooks administrator, who approves it on Intuit’s consent page. Once connected, Asung IMS reads and writes accounting records (such as customers, products and services, invoices, payments, credit memos, accounts and tax codes) so that Asung’s sales records and its books stay in step.

An administrator can disconnect Asung IMS at any time, either from within Asung IMS or from the QuickBooks Online app management page. After disconnection, Asung IMS no longer has access to the QuickBooks company.

4. Acceptable use

  • Use Asung IMS only for Asung’s business.
  • Do not try to access data or functions you are not authorized for.
  • Do not share data from Asung IMS outside Asung except as your job requires.
