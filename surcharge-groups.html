Surcharge groups

A group is a surcharge (for example a US tariff) that new sales order and POS lines pick up by themselves when their product is in the group. Put products into a group on Products (Edit) or on Product Sheet (the Surcharge group column — filter by brand or supplier and paste). Changing a group's value or turning it off only affects lines added from now on — lines already on draft orders keep what they have; clear those by hand on the order. Sets cannot be in a group (sales order lines are singles). Only an admin can change groups.
